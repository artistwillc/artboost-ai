# ArtBoost AI Frequently Asked Questions

## What is ArtBoost AI?

ArtBoost AI is a social media automation platform designed to help artists, creators, and small businesses create, schedule, and publish content across multiple social media platforms.

## Which social media platforms are supported?

ArtBoost AI currently supports:

* Pinterest
* Facebook
* Instagram
* X

Additional platforms may be added in future updates.

## How does scheduling work?

Create a campaign, select your platforms, choose a publish date and time, and ArtBoost AI will automatically publish your content when scheduled.

## Is there a free plan?

Yes. Free accounts can create up to 5 campaigns per month.

## What does Pro include?

Pro subscribers receive:

* Unlimited campaigns
* Post Everywhere functionality
* Advanced scheduling
* Priority access to new features

## How do referral rewards work?

Each successful referral earns one free month of Pro access.

Users can bank up to three free months at a time.

## Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. Subscriptions can be managed or canceled at any time through the billing portal.

## Are my social media accounts secure?

Yes. ArtBoost AI uses official platform authentication systems and does not store your social media passwords.

## Does ArtBoost AI store my images?

Images uploaded to campaigns may be securely stored to provide scheduling and publishing functionality.

## What happens if a social platform experiences an outage?

Publishing may be delayed or unavailable if the social platform's API or services are unavailable.

## Can I delete my account?

Yes. Users may request account deletion by contacting support.
